How to play

Fill the grid so that every row, every column and every three-by-three box contains the digits 1 to 9, once each. The bold digits are the clues and cannot be changed. Tap an empty cell, then tap a digit on the pad below the board. That is the whole rule; the rules page walks through a first puzzle.

What the buttons do

  • Notes switches note mode on and off. In note mode the digits you tap are written small in the cell as reminders. Fill notes writes every possible digit in every empty cell.
  • Undo takes back your last move, up to 200 moves. Erase clears the selected cell.
  • Hint works in two taps: first it names the technique and where to look, then it explains the step and offers to place the digit.
  • Check marks digits that do not match the solution. Digits that break the rule (the same digit twice in a row, column or box) turn red by themselves.
  • Pause stops the clock and hides the grid. Your game is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back.
  • Print prints the puzzle as it stands. For fresh puzzles on paper, use the print page.

On a keyboard: arrow keys move, 1 to 9 place a digit, Backspace erases, N switches notes.

Four levels, graded by logic

Every puzzle is made in your browser when you ask for it, checked to have exactly one solution, and graded by the techniques a person needs to solve it. Easy and Medium need only singles. Hard needs pairs or box-and-line work. Expert needs triples, wings or fish. No puzzle here ever needs a guess. How the grading works.

Learn the techniques

The guides explain eleven solving techniques in order, each with worked positions drawn from real puzzles: from the hidden single that solves most easy grids to the X-Wing and Swordfish. Start with cross-hatching and the solving routine.

Questions people ask

Does every sudoku have only one solution?

Every properly made one does. All puzzles generated on this site are checked by a search program to have exactly one solution before you see them. If a puzzle from another source seems to have two answers, a clue was probably copied wrongly; our solver page can test it.

Do I ever need to guess?

Not on this site. Each puzzle here is also solved, before it is shown, with the step-by-step techniques we teach, so a logical move always exists. Elsewhere, some very hard puzzles need long chains of reasoning that feel close to guessing.

Where are my game and my best times saved?

In your browser's own storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us. If you clear your browser data or use another device, the saved game and times are not there.
