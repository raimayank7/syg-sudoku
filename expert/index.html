Expert sudoku

The hardest level here: each puzzle needs a triple, an X-Wing, an XY-Wing or a Swordfish. Still pure logic, never a guess.

0:00

What an Expert puzzle needs

Each Expert puzzle on this site needs at least one step from the advanced set: a naked triple, a hidden triple, an X-Wing, an XY-Wing or a Swordfish. It will usually need several pair and locked-candidate steps as well. In our test runs the XY-Wing was by far the most frequent of the advanced steps, followed by the X-Wing; Swordfish and triples were rare.

Still no guessing

Before a puzzle is shown, the site's step-by-step solver finishes it using only the eleven techniques in our guides. Anything that would need longer chains or trial and error is rejected. So when you are stuck, a named, explainable step exists, and the Hint button can show it.

A method for Expert

  1. Take the singles, write full notes, and do the Hard routine: pointing pairs, box-line reductions, naked and hidden pairs in every unit.
  2. Look at cells with two candidates. Find three that link as XY, XZ and YZ, with the first seeing the other two: an XY-Wing. Remove Z from cells that see both wings.
  3. Take one digit at a time and look only at its candidates. Two rows where it has two cells each, in the same columns, make an X-Wing.
  4. In units with five or more empty cells, look for three cells that hold only three digits between them.
  5. If none of these appears, check the Swordfish for digits with many scattered candidates.

Keep your notes exact

At this level one wrong note is fatal, because the advanced patterns depend on a candidate being truly absent. Remove a candidate only when you have checked the whole condition, and remove it at once. If you doubt your notes, tap Check to confirm that your placed digits are right, then Fill notes to rebuild the simple candidates and re-apply your removals.

Expect to pause

An Expert puzzle has long quiet stretches. That is the level working as intended. Twenty minutes or more is ordinary, and a single step may take five. Use Pause if you need to leave; the clock stops and the grid is saved in your browser.

If you get stuck

Tap Hint once to learn which technique comes next and in which unit. That narrows the search without spoiling it. The second tap shows the cells, the reasoning and every candidate to remove.

Beyond this level

Harder puzzles exist, using chains and colouring. We do not make them, because we teach only what we can explain step by step. See how difficulty is graded.
