Sudoku

Printable sudoku puzzles

Fresh puzzles for paper, at any level, with answers if you want them. Made in your browser each time.

How to print

  1. Choose a level, how many puzzles you want and how many should go on each page.
  2. Tick "Add an answer sheet" if you want the solutions on a separate last page.
  3. Tap Make puzzles. New puzzles are generated in your browser; it takes a second or two for the harder levels.
  4. Tap Print. Your browser's print window opens. Choose your printer, or "Save as PDF" to keep the sheet as a file.

Only the puzzles are printed: the page header, these instructions and all buttons are left out. Each time you tap Make puzzles you get a different set.

Which layout to choose

Per pageBest for
1Large print, readers who want big digits, room for pencil marks in Hard and Expert puzzles
2Everyday solving on A4 paper, with enough room for notes
4Easy and Medium puzzles, classroom sheets, saving paper
6Mini 4 by 4 and 6 by 6 puzzles for children, or a travel sheet of Easy puzzles

Tips for a clean print

  • Use portrait (upright) paper. The layouts are made for A4 and also fit Letter size.
  • If the grid lines look faint, switch off "economy" or "draft" mode in the printer settings.
  • In the print window, set margins to "Default" and leave "Headers and footers" off if you do not want the page address printed at the edge.
  • Black and white is enough; the sheets use no colour.

Ideas for using printed puzzles

  • Journeys. A sheet of four and a pencil need no battery and no signal.
  • Classrooms. Print mini puzzles for early finishers, with the answer sheet kept at the teacher's desk. See the kids page for how to introduce the rule.
  • Elders. One puzzle per page gives large, clear digits.
  • A family contest. Print the same sheet for everyone (make it once and print several copies) and start together.
  • Practice without help. On paper there is no Hint button and no red warning. It is the best test of whether a level has really been mastered.

About the puzzles

Each puzzle is made by the same generator as the puzzles you play on this site: a full grid is filled at random, clues are removed in mirror pairs while the solution stays unique, and the result is graded by the techniques needed. So a printed Hard puzzle really needs a pair or box-and-line step, and every printed puzzle has exactly one answer. How the levels are graded.

You may print as many sheets as you like for yourself, your family, your class or your club, free of charge.
