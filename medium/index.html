Medium sudoku

About 23 to 32 clues. Still solvable with singles only, but you must look harder for them.

0:00

What changes from Easy

A Medium puzzle here has as few clues as the generator can leave, usually between 23 and 32. The surprising part is that it needs no new technique. Every step is still a hidden single or a naked single. What changes is how many moves are open at a time: often only one or two, and they are in less obvious places.

Where the moves hide

  • Along rows and columns. In Easy puzzles nearly every single can be found by scanning boxes. In Medium ones you must also ask, for a row or column, where each missing digit can go.
  • In cells with one candidate. A naked single in an open part of the grid is hard to see by eye, because its eight ruled-out digits are spread across a row, a column and a box. Notes help.
  • After a quiet spell. The start is often slow, with a few placements, and then the grid opens up all at once.

A method for Medium

  1. Do full rounds of cross-hatching through the digits until a round finds nothing.
  2. Check each row and column that has four or fewer gaps: list its missing digits and test each gap.
  3. If nothing appears, tap Fill notes (or write notes yourself in the most crowded band) and look for a cell with one candidate.
  4. Return to scanning after every placement.

Should you use notes?

You can finish a Medium puzzle without them, and trying to is good training for the eye. Notes make naked singles easy to find, at the price of a busier board. A middle way is to note only cells that have two candidates. See pencil marks.

If you get stuck

The first tap on Hint says whether the next move is a hidden or a naked single and which unit it is in. That is usually enough. If you find you need hints for naked singles again and again, play a few games with full notes until you start to sense where they are.

When to move up

When Medium feels routine, Hard introduces the first steps that remove candidates instead of placing digits. Read about pointing pairs before your first Hard game.
