Hard sudoku

Singles are not enough. Each puzzle needs at least one pointing pair, box-line reduction, naked pair or hidden pair.

0:00

What makes these Hard

Every Hard puzzle on this site reaches a point where no single is available. To go on, you must remove a candidate with one of four techniques: a pointing pair, a box-line reduction, a naked pair or a hidden pair. The generator checks this: a puzzle that could be finished with singles alone is not served as Hard, and neither is one that needs anything more advanced.

In our test runs, pointing pairs were needed far more often than the other three. If you learn one technique for this level, learn that.

You need notes

These techniques work on candidates, so pencil marks are no longer optional. Take the singles first, then tap Fill notes or write the notes yourself. Keep them accurate: on this board, placing a digit removes it from the notes of the cells that see it, but notes removed by a technique you must take out yourself. Switch Notes on, select the cell and tap the digit to remove it.

A method for Hard

  1. Scan for singles until none is left.
  2. With notes in place, go box by box and digit by digit: are the candidates for this digit in this box all on one row or column? If so, remove the digit from the rest of that line.
  3. Go line by line: are the candidates for a digit in this row or column all inside one box? If so, clean the rest of the box.
  4. Look for two cells in a unit with the same two candidates, and then for two digits that share the same two cells.
  5. After any removal, check for a new single before looking for another pattern.

What a typical game looks like

You place digits steadily, the moves dry up, you spend a few minutes finding one pattern, and the removal releases a run of singles. Many Hard puzzles need only one or two such steps. The skill is in finding them without wasting time on patterns that are not there.

If you get stuck

The first tap on Hint names the technique and the box or line, such as "first you need a pointing pair". Look for it yourself before the second tap, which shows the cells and the reasoning. Each technique name in a hint links to its guide.

When to move up

When you can find pairs and locked candidates without a hint, try Expert, after reading about triples and the XY-Wing.
