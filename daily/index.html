Daily sudoku

One puzzle per level for every date, the same for everyone. Solve today's and share your time.

0:00

How the daily puzzle works

Each date has its own set of four puzzles, one at each level. They are not downloaded from a list. The date and the level are turned into a starting number, and the generator in your browser builds the puzzle from that number. The same number always gives the same grid, so everyone who opens this page on the same date sees the same puzzles. That makes a daily grid something you can share: solve it, send your time to a friend or a family group, and compare.

The date is the one on your own device. If a friend in another time zone opens the page while their calendar shows a different date, they will see that date's puzzle; pick the same date in the box above the board to play the same one.

Missed a day?

Choose any earlier date in the date box and that day's puzzles appear, exactly as they were. There is no limit on how far back you go. Future dates are not offered.

Making it a habit

  • Pick one level and stay with it for a week. You will see your time fall as your scanning becomes regular.
  • Play at the same time each day. Many people keep the daily grid for the morning tea or the journey home.
  • No hints on the daily. If you are stuck, leave it and come back; the game is saved in this browser. A solution found after a break is worth more than a hint.
  • Share the time, not the answer. After you finish, the Share button sends your time and a link to the same puzzle. It never reveals any digits.

A group challenge

For a family or office group: agree on a level, everyone plays the daily puzzle before a fixed hour, and each posts a screenshot of the finished board with the time. Keep a simple table for the week. Use Medium if the group has mixed experience; nobody is shut out and the times stay close.

Good to know

Your daily game is kept separately from your other games, so playing a random puzzle on the home page does not disturb it. "Play it again" on a finished daily grid clears your entries and restarts the clock on the same puzzle. If the site's generator is ever changed, puzzles for past dates may change with it; we will avoid that unless a fault needs fixing.
