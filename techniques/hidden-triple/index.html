Hidden triple

Three digits fit in only the same three cells of a unit, so those cells drop all other notes. Level: Expert.

A hidden triple is to a hidden pair what a naked triple is to a naked pair. In one unit, three digits can go only in three cells, and they are the same three cells for all three digits. Those cells must take the three digits, so every other candidate in them can be removed.

Hidden triples are among the hardest of the basic patterns to see, because the three cells are usually full of notes and, as with naked triples, not every digit has to appear in every one of the three cells.

How to spot it

  1. Take a unit with at least six empty cells.
  2. For each missing digit, note the cells where it can go. Keep the digits that have two or three possible cells.
  3. Look for three of those digits whose possible cells, put together, make exactly three cells.
  4. Remove all other candidates from those three cells.
  5. The three cells now form a naked triple, which may give more removals in a second unit.

Why it works

Three digits must be placed in the unit, and between them they have only three cells to go to. Each cell holds one digit, so the three cells are completely taken by the three digits. Nothing else fits.

Another example

A second position. Checking a hidden triple is easier than finding one: take each of the three digits in turn and confirm that it appears nowhere in the unit outside the three cells.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Counting a digit that has a fourth possible cell. All three digits must be confined to the same three cells.
  • Looking cell by cell. Search by digit. Make a small table for the unit: digit, and the cells it can use.
  • Spending long on it too early. Hidden triples are rare. Try pointing pairs, box-line reduction and pairs in every unit first.

When to look for it

Use it on the most crowded unit of a stuck Expert puzzle. If a row has seven empty cells and nothing simpler works, a hidden triple (or its partner, a naked group among the remaining cells) is a real possibility.

Practise it

Play a puzzle at the Expert level, where this technique is needed. The first tap on Hint tells you when it is the step to look for.
